Hello, my name is Mohamed. I have recently finished my PhD in applied mathematics. My phd was in the field of queueing theory
more specificaly in the establishement of what is called diffusion approximations. Those are analytic approximations for queueing systems with general distributions and that are in heavy traffic regime i.e congestion.

I am mostly interested in **queueing theory** and more specificaly to its applications in cloud computing and more generaly
computer networking. 



Queueing theory
======
- Fluid and diffusion approximation for queueing systems
- Stochastic modeling of cloud computing systems using the framework of queueing theory
